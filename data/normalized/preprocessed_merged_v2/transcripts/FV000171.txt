Something I've always found very weird about being a prosthetics user is I am literally a walking advertisement for a number of different companies. We got Oser up here, on the back of my ankle is a giant blue logo for Folaua who makes my ankle. We got more writing on the side, and while I absolutely understand there is a need for labeling medical devices, these are also our body parts, and it seems very weird that there isn't an opt-out or advertising other people's companies. It's like a tattoo you didn't agree to.
