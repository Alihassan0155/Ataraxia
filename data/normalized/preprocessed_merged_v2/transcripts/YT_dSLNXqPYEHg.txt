Come with me as I host an end of summer nostalgia party that will take you back to being a kid again. I made four super simple spreads that are crowd-pleasing, interactive, and so much fun for an outdoor party. When I think about nostalgic summers as a kid, my mind immediately goes to snow cones. I wanted to recreate those iconic snow cone flavors, but without all the chemicals and food colorings. I started with a wild blueberry lemon syrup that is naturally colored a deep purple blue and has a bright acidic tang from the lemon. For my red snow cone syrup, I wanted to do a combination of strawberries and raspberries. How did you? water and a drizzle of blackberry balsamic vinegar to give it just a touch of complexity and make it a little more grown up. lastly for yellow, i did a mango lime syrup. i wrapped the lime zest into sugar first and then sprinkled it over frozen mango and reduced it down like the rest of the syrups. i added a squeeze of fresh lime and i think this might have been my favorite flavor of the bunch. then i assembled our snow cone bar with some spoons, paper cones, an ice cream scoop, and my three syrup flavors. i set the bar up alongside this snow cone machine we bought last summer. these rainbow snow cones turned out so pretty and were way more delicious than what i had as a kid.
