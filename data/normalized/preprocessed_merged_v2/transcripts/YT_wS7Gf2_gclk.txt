Some exciting new details about the sequel to the Arcane series have been leaked. While some of it was expected, like how the story will follow Mel and prominently feature Swain, we also got a surprising reveal. The show's title is Iron and Chains. What's even more intriguing is that the story will reportedly tie into the upcoming Ambessa novel. Pages from this novel, recently posted on Reddit, suggest it will explore the backstory of League champion Rel, while implying that how Mel got her powers is also tied to her.
