I recently filled up this bottle, and it's actually still kind of warm. What I think is really interesting though, is that when I shake it, it turns red, and if I shake it a bit more, it becomes completely green. In my opinion, this honestly kind of looks like magic, but it's actually just because it contains a dye called indigo carmine. Whenever I shook it, The dye was able to react with the air that was in the bottle, and this caused it to change colors. What I also think is interesting though, is that if I just let it sit here for a bit, the color will slowly change again. This is because along with the indigo carmine, I had also dissolved in some glucose and sodium hydroxide, which can slowly reverse the reaction that it had with the air. Eventually, it'll go back to being yellow, and it's ready to be shaken again.
