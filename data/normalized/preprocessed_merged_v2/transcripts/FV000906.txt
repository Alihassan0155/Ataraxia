Welcome to the Ramen Museum in Yokohama. The first floor seems to be an ordinary museum, but downstairs is where you really want to be. Here you'll find a bunch of shops serving up all sorts of ramen for you to try. There are beautifully recreated streets to walk down where you can find more than just ramen. You can order the ramen you want from these machines outside the shops. We ordered a Chashu Ramen and a Soy Sauce Ramen, but they have loads of other ones for you to choose from. And of course, why not top it off with dessert and some ice cream? If you want to get a taste of Japan's history, I can't recommend this place enough.
