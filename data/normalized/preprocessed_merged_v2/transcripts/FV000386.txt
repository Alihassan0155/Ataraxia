Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton is the West Coast's premier expeditionary training base. With over 125,000 acres of land, Camp Pendleton is home to many endangered species and one-of-a-kind training areas. With its 17 miles of coastline being the last undeveloped coastline in Southern California, Camp Pendleton offers many resources for military training and endangered species protection. Camp Pendleton takes pride in protecting the environment while showcasing its world-class military training. Recycling and proper waste disposal are a big part of preserving the special ecosystem.
