Growing up in North Korea is disturbing. Here are three reasons why. Number one, kids must learn to say thank you our leaders before they can even say mom or dad as the Kim family is considered greater than anyone else. Number two, this North Korean defector told me that starting at age eight he had to join mandatory scouts and study regime propaganda until he became a teenager. Number three, children pray to portraits of the Kim family every morning as they are brainwashed into working worshiping their leaders like gods.
