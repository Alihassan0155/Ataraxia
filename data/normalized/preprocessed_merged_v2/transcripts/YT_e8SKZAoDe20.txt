To protect her 18-year-old daughter's innocence, the mother applied charcoal dust to her face every day to hide her beauty. However, a pitch-black face terrified people. The villagers saw Lucy as if she were plagued. Not only did they destroy and burn the stall she relied on for her livelihood, but they even attempted to pour hot water on her. In the midst of the crisis, her mother swiftly stepped forward and knocked the hot pan to the ground. The villagers swarmed around the mother and daughter, but they were momentarily stunned by the woman's fearlessness. So much so, that no one could utter a single word out of fear, and they allowed Lucy to leave. When they returned home, the mother apologized to her daughter as she wiped the charcoal from her face. The younger sibling, unable to bear the fear he had just experienced, protested that their mother should not have deliberately made his sister look ugly. However, the mother saying, Have you forgotten what happened to your older sister? And slapped him hard.
