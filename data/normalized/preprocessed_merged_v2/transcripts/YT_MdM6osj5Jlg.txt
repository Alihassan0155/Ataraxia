You, it seems, are the manifestation of several lifetimes of toil. Brown v. bored in the flesh. Most days the classroom feels like an antechamber. You are deemed expert on all things Morrison, King, Malcolm, Rosa. Hell, weren't you sitting on that bus too? You are everybody's best friend until you are not. Hip-hop lyricologist. Presumed athlete. Free and reduced sideshow. Exception and caricature. too black and too white all at once. If you are successful, it is because of affirmative action. If you fail, it is because you were destined to. You are invisible until they turn on the Friday night lights. Here you are star before they render you asteroid. Before they watch you turn to dust.
