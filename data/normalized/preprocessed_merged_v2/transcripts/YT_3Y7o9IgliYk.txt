Why is the 600-ton steel ball hanging from the ceiling in Taipei 101, Taiwan's tallest building? It's because Taiwan sits right on the ring of fire, home of 90% of the world's earthquakes. When you build one of the tallest buildings on earth here, shaking isn't a rare emergency, it's a weekly occurrence. Which is why the solution wasn't to fight the movement, it was to work with it. Because normally if you were to add a fixed weight to the top of something, you would just increase its instability, making it way more top-heavy. However, this weight isn't fixed. it's suspended allowing essentially 100 elephants to swing freely perfectly counteracting the natural sway of the building to keep it in place in hurricanes and earthquakes
