Three different types of curls using the same curling iron. The first type is going to be a big bouncy glam wave. Start by heating the strand and then move the curling iron all the way to the ends. Roll all the way up to the top, keeping the curling iron parallel to the floor. Release the curl into your hand and hold for a few seconds to cool. Once released, you can see that this leaves a big bouncy curl. The next style is going to be a beach wave. For this one, you want to keep the curling iron at a 45 degree angle to the floor. You start at the base of the strand, curling the hair around, and and then release the top as you move down the strand all the way down. way to the ends. This gives a very beachy textured look. The final style is a spiral curl. For this one you hold the curling iron parallel to the floor and simply wrap the hair around letting it twist as you go. Hold for a few seconds then release in your hand to cool. Once it's cooled you have a beautiful spiral curl. This one can be brushed and teased to make a Hollywood wave.
