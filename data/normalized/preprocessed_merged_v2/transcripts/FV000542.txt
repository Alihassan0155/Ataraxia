Water is everywhere so we need to manage it. Our water source might run out soon so you have to save it. Here are examples on ways to save water. Refuse water from washing dishes to flush the toilet. You can also reuse water from washing the rice to water the plants. Check pipes and faucets for leaks to prevent water from being wasted. If the leaks are severe, call a plumber. Those are some ways to save our water. So remember, water is vital so let's clean and save it together.
