My wife was in the mood for a healthy snack, so I made her these cheesy air fryer sweet potato puffs. Super delicious and simple to make. Start by taking out all your angonian sweet potatoes and stabbing them with a fork. Wrap in foil and bake these until soft. Scrape out the insides and mash with a fork. Then add your flour, mozzarella cheese and cheddar cheese. Mix this together, shape into balls and air fry for around 6 minutes at 200 degrees Celsius.
