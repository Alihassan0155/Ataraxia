In the 1240s, the Franciscan friar John de Plano Carpini traveled as a papal envoy to the Mongols. Taking careful notes, he made these suggestions to Europeans to fight the Mongols. All kingdoms must be prepared to help their neighbor, for no single power can stand against them and they must be fought without delay. For equipment, they must bring quality bows and crossbows, with arrows hard enough to pierce Mongol armor, as well as long axes and spears, and good armor. on armored must stay behind and shoot arrows. They should copy the decimal organization of the Mongols, and the commanders should stay out of the line of battle to direct the army. The army must work together and none break rank or pursue fleeing Mongols. Finally, they should look for people's force to fight for the Mongols. Karpini believed that should they be freed from Mongol service, they would be willing to fight against them.
