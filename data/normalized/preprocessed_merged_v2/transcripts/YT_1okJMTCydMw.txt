Wednesday and Enid venture into a haunted clockmaker's workshop, where ghostly chimes reveal ethereal zombie clockworkers fixing enchanted clocks. As gears start working mysteriously, Wednesday uses her expertise in mechanics to pinpoint which faulty gear is casting supernatural chaos. Enid, with her comedic antics, keeps the clockworkers distracted, providing comic relief during tense moments. Together they swap the cursed gear with an enchanted decoy that disrupts the spectral work of the eye. The clockworkers dissolve into smoke, the enchanting allure dispersed into the ether. Their strategy lifts the curse, ensuring the workshop returns to tranquility, blending teamwork, suspense, humor, and perfect timing.
