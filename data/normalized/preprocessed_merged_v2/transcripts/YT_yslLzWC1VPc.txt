Rent prices in London are insane, so people have to get creative. Here are the three craziest places that people actually live in London. Number one, a tiny home built inside a dumpster. Yes, a literal dumpster. It's the only way that I was able to live alone in London. It's insulated, has electricity, and even a lofted bed. The guy who made it says he built it in three weeks, and he's lived here for two years with no shower. Number two, a boathouse on the canal. Thousands of Londoners actually live like this, floating between neighborhoods, paying small moving fees instead of crazy rent. It's peaceful until you have to empty the toilet. That's where all my poop and piss are. And number three, a janitor's closet turned into a micro apartment. It's only about 60 square feet. Basically a lofted bed, a sink, and not much else.
