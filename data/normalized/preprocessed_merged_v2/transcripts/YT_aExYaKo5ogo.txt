Something you might not expect happens when you slightly redesign a handle. The vast majority of handles look kind of like this, right? Well, this is what we like to call a grip-based handle, and it works fine for many people. But when you slightly tweak the handle to look something more like this, it changes how your fingers interact with the handle. Instead of a design that's based on your grip, this is based on better leverage. And that better leverage makes it incredibly easy to hold securely without slipping. And it's one of those things that most people honestly don't really need, but when you try it a few times, it's hard to go back.
