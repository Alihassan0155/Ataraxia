In the age of social media, viral prank networks once captured the imagination of millions. Initially celebrated for their creativity, these networks thrived on platforms like YouTube and Instagram. With pranks ranging from harmless gags to more controversial stunts, they amassed a colossal following. However, as the line between entertainment and ethical considerations blurred, criticism began to rise. The audience, once enthralled, started questioning the morality and impact of these pranks. Consequently, many networks had to adapt, pivoting towards more responsible content, marking a shift in the online prank phenomenon.
