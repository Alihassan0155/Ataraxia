This story is truly heartwarming. The man rescued a trapped lizard. After helping to pull out the lizard, he immediately ran away. But a few days later, a lizard lay in front of the man's door, as if he wanted to enter the house. The man recognized that this was the lizard he had saved before, so he let him stay at home. The lizard soon became familiar with the man's home and was also used to being touched by him. He even became a good friend with the cat at home. After getting along with him for a period of time, The man was surprised. The lizard actually knew that he went to the toilet. He often fed him his favorite vegetables. When he took him out for a walk, he would be very happy. He also liked to listen to the music played by the man. But sometimes he would be a little naughty and tried to take a bite of the plants at home. These kind-hearted people definitely deserve a like.
