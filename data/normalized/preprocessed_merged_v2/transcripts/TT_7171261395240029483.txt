So every winter, my husband builds our kids an ice skating rink right in the front yard at our house. This year, I thought I'd show you how he does it. So first he builds this giant frame, then he lays a liner over top of it. He fills the liner with water straight from our garden hose. And while the water is filling up, he pins the sides of the liner to the frame. While the water is freezing, he puts lights all the way around it and sets up my heater. We love it so much and the kids have so much fun skating all winter long.
