The FNAF easter egg in Bendy and the Dark Revival that everyone missed. In Chapter 5, after being abducted by Alice Angel in Wilson's Mansion, she takes us to her party and to escape we must solve a riddle. To solve the puzzle, you need to put four pictures of different animals together in a very specific order, and hints to the correct order are provided by the other party attendees around the table. The solution to the puzzle and correct order of the animals turns out to be the rabbit first, and then the bear, followed by the bird, and lastly the fox. FNAF fans should immediately recognize this order as it's actually the stage positions of the animatronic characters as seen in the first game. A bear in the middle, with a rabbit and bird off to its side, and lastly a fox hiding just a short distance away. So now you know about...
