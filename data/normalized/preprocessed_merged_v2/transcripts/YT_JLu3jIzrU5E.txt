You've played through Cyberpunk 2077 Samurai related quests and side content, but did you catch this cameo in these quests? Along your travels in Cyberpunk you'll meet Samurai superfan Kareem Noel, who tells you about his fandom and history attending Samurai concerts. In the main quest we get a flashback to a gig that Samurai does right before the assault on Arasaka Towers, and we can see Kareem is in fact in the front row, looking like the happiest man on planet earth. There's also a rare possibility to see him during the quest like Supreme, where V performs with Samurai with Silverhand borrowing his body. Looks like Kareem really is as devoted as he says he is. Neat little tie in and a detail you likely missed.
