I told you lots of fun at Finnegan's Wake. One morning Tim got rather full. His head felt heavy which made him shake. Fell from a ladder and broke his skull. And they carried him home, his corpse to wake. Rolled him up in a nice clean sheet. And laid him out upon the bed. A bottle of whiskey at his feet. And a barrel of porter at his hen.
