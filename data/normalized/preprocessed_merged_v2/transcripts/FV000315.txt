I got to tour the NFL's headquarters. After security was the lobby with a helmet wall and every single Super Bowl ring rotating on an LED screen. They even had a broadcast booth up front. The helmet wall lined the second level of security. This place was like Fort Knox. Inside was all of the meeting rooms and cubicles, as well as the broadcast booths for the NFL's TV shows. My favorite was the mural walls through every hallway lined with amazing art of NFL goats. And the backyard was insane with a view of SoFi and a 40-yard field for all the employees.
