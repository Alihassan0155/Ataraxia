What I have here are a bunch of diamonds that I bought online a few years ago. They're all uncut and they're quite small, but I still think they're pretty nice. What I think is really cool though, is what happens if I put them all in a quartz tube and start blasting them with a blowtorch. As they warmed up, they started glowing red, and normally this is all that would happen. However, in this case, I'm also shooting in some pure oxygen. In general, diamonds are usually pretty resilient, but under these conditions, they're actually getting vaporized. They're all being turned into carbon dioxide gas, and after about 40 minutes, all of my diamonds had disappeared.
