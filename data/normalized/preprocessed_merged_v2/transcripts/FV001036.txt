Did rock guitarist and member of the band Fleetwood Mac, Lindsay Buckingham, throw his guitar at fellow band member Stevie Nicks? In a 2013 interview in the Daily Mail, Nicks recalls, It was the Tusk Tour, 1980, Auckland, New Zealand. I was doing my thing with my shawl, and Lindsay pulled his jacket up over his head and started mimicking me behind my back. I thought, well, that's not working for me. But I didn't do anything. This must have infuriated him because he came over and kicked me. And I'd never had anyone be physical with me in my life. Then he picked up a Black Les Paul guitar and he just frisbeated at me. He missed. I ducked. But he could have killed me.
