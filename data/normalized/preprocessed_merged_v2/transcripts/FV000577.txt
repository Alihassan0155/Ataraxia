I probably enjoyed playing Mr. Bean the most because he's a character who's furthest away from my own character. As a person, I don't like him at all. I think he's very odd and pretty weird and not very nice. What's fun about it is it's an escape. You know, when you play the character, you don't care what you do. It's a weird kind of release. The first time we did the character, I'm fairly certain, was 1979 on stage. And that was when we had developed, I think, two sketches. One was the beach sketch in which he's trying to change into a swimming trunks on the beach. And then there's the one in the church in which he's sitting in the church and he misbehaves while someone's spouting a sermon.
