Hello, I'm a droplet of water. I'm only a few millimeters wide and I just came out of the end of that needle onto a boiling hot sheet of silicon. Even though the surface is well over 100 degrees Celsius, I'm able to bounce and glide on top of the silicon. This is known as the Leidenfrost effect. Because of the immense heat, my underside immediately boils, but an incredibly small layer of steam is being trapped neatly between me and the silicon, insulating my con- from heating up and evaporating. However, if I happen to slip off of the silicon and land on a rougher or slightly cooler surface, I'd boil immediately. But hopefully that won't happen. I'm already liquid. I'm evaporating!
