The Modular is a great touring helmet because you can flip up the lid, the chin bar, have a coffee, have a smoke, whatever you need to do, and easily hop back on the bike, slam that thing down, you're ready to go. The Neotec 3 has got some slight updates. It's not a major overhaul over the Neotec 2, but there are some features and things here that are just slight revisions that I think are going to make a big impact for those using the helmet. The US gets a DOT-only version, which means it's going to be slightly heavier than its ECE counterpart. That's just what we'll have to deal with over here but the helmet still has the AIM fiberglass organic shell compound and you're going to get three shell sizes with this helmet. Extra small through medium are one shell size, large gets its own shell, and then XL through 2XL is another shell size.
