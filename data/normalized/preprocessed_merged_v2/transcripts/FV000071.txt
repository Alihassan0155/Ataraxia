We were so poor when I was growing up that Daddy would take us to town to the kicking place just so we could lick other people's fingers. And if Mama opened a pan of pork and beans we would fight over the fat. I had a brother that was almost deaf. Sometimes Mama would sit us down at the table and just read the recipe. He almost starved to death because he couldn't hear a word. Whenever someone would come up in the driveway, Daddy would send us to the driveway and make us bark because we couldn't even afford a dog.
