As Queen of England, she had eight children with Henry II and devoted the rest of her life to setting them up in politically advantageous positions. Three would become King of England, one Queen of Castile, and another Queen of Sicily, marriages she negotiated personally. And they did not make it easy on her. When her sons rebelled against their father, her support for them got her in prison for 16 years. Then during the reign of her son, Richard the Lionheart, he ditched England immediately after his coronation to go on crusade, leaving Eleanor to run things and keep his younger brother. John from seizing the throne. Then when Richard got captured, she raised the money for his ransom. And after Richard's death, she forgave John and loyally defended his throne into her 80s, even against her own grandchildren. But Henry wasn't her first husband. See, originally she'd been the Queen of France before annulling that marriage to become Queen of England. And to do that, she had to agree to never see her two daughters again.
