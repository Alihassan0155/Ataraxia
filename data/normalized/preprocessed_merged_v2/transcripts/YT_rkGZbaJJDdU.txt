I met this tribe in Zimbabwe where everyone has two toes on each foot. They're called the Vadoma tribe and it was a two-day journey on long dirt roads to reach them. We stopped in a shop to buy some goods for them that we delivered and we showed up and we were in the middle of nowhere. They sleep in these little huts. So we tried to make friends right away. We hung out with some guys. They showed us how they play soccer, they climb trees and they just explained how that's literally how they're born. It's a really interesting genetic abnormality that I've never seen before.
