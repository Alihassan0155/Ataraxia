I think that people really downplay how big of a factor skin is, especially when it comes to mental health. When you're experiencing acne, it feels like that's all the world can see. Even if they don't, that's how it feels. You get obsessed with trying to get rid of it, try a million products, and in the end, most of the time it only makes it worse. The progress pictures are endless, and you find yourself scrolling through them aimlessly, trying to find something different. Any little change to prove you're on the right path. It is exhausting.
