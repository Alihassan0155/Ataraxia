Do not pray prayers based on your ability. Do not pray prayers based on your understanding. I think sometimes people refuse to pray certain things because they dismiss it as an impossibility. We pray prayers that are limited to our understanding, limited to our ability. We think, well, I don't want to pray something that's too far, or I don't want to pray something that's too difficult. Why? Because we're measuring our standard of faith based upon man's ability rather than God's ability. When you pray, pray prayers that only God can answer. Type amen if you agree.
