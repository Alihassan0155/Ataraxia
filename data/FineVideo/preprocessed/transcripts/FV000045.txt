I was invited back to another Dino Alive Safari, but this time there were some surprises. The first one being this Stegosaur that pooped. Whitney enjoyed that. The Pachycephalosaurus was a new dino that would go around headbutting the actors. Very cute. The Chunky Enchynosaur was also here, as well as the absolute unit that is the Brachiosaurus. And of course there were dinosaur fights. This Allosaur still had not learned its lesson from the last time. The T-Rex came out to show the Allosaur how it's done, as well as to chomp on a few people. Then a fight between two trikes and two rexes broke out, before they were interrupted by the new star of the show, the Giganotosaurus. So a final fight between two T-Rexes and the Giga commenced. It was hard to say who was winning, before even more dinosaurs entered the stage, giving everyone a chance to see these amazing creations. If you're a dinosaur fan and you happen to be in Japan during summer, I'd highly recommend checking this out.
