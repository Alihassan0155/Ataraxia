The Holy Spirit protects you from harmful relationships. David and his men, defectors from Saul's authority, were hiding from Saul in the town of Ziklag. The warriors who defended the future king had formed a hedge around him. David was insulated, covered, no enemies could approach. David was then approached by more men from the tribes of Benjamin and Judah. He knew not whether they were there to join his ranks or to take his life, and how could he have known? For all David knew, the men who approached him could have been spies sent by Saul. Then the Spirit came upon Amasai, the leader of the 30, and he said, We are yours, David. We are on your side, son of Jesse. So David let them join him, and he made them officers over his troops. It was the Holy Spirit upon the men that confirmed to David that they were for him, not against him. The Holy Spirit will protect your inner circle by closely connecting you with people of a kindred spirit. The Holy Spirit in you knows the Holy Spirit in them.
