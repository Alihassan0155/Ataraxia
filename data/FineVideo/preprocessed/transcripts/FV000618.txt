Hi, Kabal. Do you know what's special today? Yes, Ali. Today is National Resource Conservation Day. Every year, India observes National Energy Conservation on December 14. Today is organized by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency, which operates under the Ministry of Power, aiming to present India's better achievement in cost-efficient energy production and resource conservation. National Energy Conservation Day aims to generate mass awareness in India about the importance of saving energy. Citizens are informed to minimize wastage to conserve resources.
