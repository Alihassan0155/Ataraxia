This fake FNAF theory is real? Yep, one of the most popular FNAF theories is actually true. Kind of, let me explain. When FNAF 1 was released, many people speculated that Foxy was actually a good guy, and that when he jumpscared you, he actually was checking up on you, and accidentally kills you from a heart attack, which led to the very popular Foxy is a good guy theory. But after years passed, this theory actually became true in FNAF Ultimate Custom Night. In FNAF Ultimate Custom Night, there was only two Foxys, with that being FNAF 1 Foxy and Rockstar Foxy. And guess what? Rockstar Foxy is the only character in the game that actually helps the player, which makes this theory true.
