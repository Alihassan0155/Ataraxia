150 years ago there were possibly over 2 million giraffes. By 2015 that number was down to only 80,000. 21 countries, governments, and nonprofits including the Giraffe Conservation Foundation got to work. Recent data says the current giraffe population is around 117,000, approximately 20% higher than in 2015. Habitat loss, poaching, and the changing climate still threaten them. Seeing the giraffe populations rise as a result of this creation care work is giving new hope to conservationists.
