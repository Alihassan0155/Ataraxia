Say hello to my favorite carry-on luggage, the Sterling Pacific 35L Cabin Bag. It looks luxe and feels super solid thanks to the 5052 all aluminum construction reinforced with A380 aluminum corners. The Italian full grain leather handles are buttery smooth, the two oversized through-axle wheels and retractable aluminum trolley are incredibly sturdy and give you maximum control, and there's the immensely satisfying sound of the TSA locks. It gets even better inside. A ring-spun twill weave lining adds to its lush and luxe appearance, plenty of space. The amenity and repair kit doubles nicely as a dop kit. Everything is secured via the interjecting buckle straps, and there's extra storage space in the mesh compartment on the lid. As someone who appreciates quality, attention to detail, and spec-driven design, this is made for pilots and frequent flyers after all, the Sterling Pacific 35L Cabin Bag is the perfect and most stylish aluminum luggage I've ever used.
