We live in Koz, a Greek island in the southeastern Aegean Sea. Birthplace of Hippocrates, father of medicine and author of the Hippocratic Oath. During these challenging times, we stand in awe of the incredible bravery and devotion of all doctors and nurses as they fight alongside their patients, just like Hippocrates did at the sacred Asclepion. While scientists transcend boundaries to find a cure, it is time for us to help by staying home until science triumphs once again, until humanity reunites. We will rise above this strong land. We will meet again, soon.
