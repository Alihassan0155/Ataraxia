Here's my Dubai trip in less than 60 seconds. Starting with Burj Khalifa. The view from the top, 38 pounds. Next, the fat horse by Fernando Botero. Beautiful piece. The Dubai fountains. Shows are from 6pm till 11pm. Sheikh Zayed Brand Mosque. Free, but make sure you dress modestly. ATV rides in the desert. Best thing ever, highly recommended. Dune Bashik at sunset. Gorgeous and fun. Dubai Marina Yacht Tour. Live a little and enjoy the views. Definitely worth it. Drobo village, a bit of a hit and miss Fun but overwhelming, not for everyone. Any rooftop pool, period. Abu Dhabi's heritage village, see the traditional Emirati way of life back in the day. The Gold Souk, only if you're buying gold, museum of the future, didn't like it, skip it. The Dubai Grand Souk, a bit of a tourist trap, won't recommend it. Medina Jumeirah, the best market in Dubai, that one I liked a lot and the area around it is just lovely.
