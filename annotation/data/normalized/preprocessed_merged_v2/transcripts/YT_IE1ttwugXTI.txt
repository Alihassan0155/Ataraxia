Breaking these three laws in North Korea isn't just illegal, they can end your life. Number one, bring home local currency. You can actually exchange your money to North Korean won, but you're not allowed to take it out of the country as a souvenir or you will face years in prison for smuggling state property. Number two, losing your passport. If you misplace it, there's no embassy in North Korea to help you get a new one. Without your passport, you can't leave the country and you'll be detained immediately. Number three, spreading foreign information. When you enter and leave North Korea, you must tell the immigration officers how many sim cards you have. If you lose one, you'll be accused of sharing information about the outside world to the locals.
