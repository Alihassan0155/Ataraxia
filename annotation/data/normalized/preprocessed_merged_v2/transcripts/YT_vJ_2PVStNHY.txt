When you're in danger or extreme excitement, your brain sends a signal to your adrenal glands. They release adrenaline, also known as epinephrine, straight into your bloodstream. Your heart rate spikes. Your airways open. Blood flows to your muscles. Pupils dilate. Suddenly, you're stronger, faster, more alert. This is the fight-or-flight response built into us for survival. It's what lets a mother lift a car to save her child. or a base jumper leap off a cliff without hesitation. Adrenaline doesn't just protect, it pushes your limits. So next time you feel that rush, don't panic. That's evolution giving you a boost. Feel the thrill. Embrace the power. That's adrenaline.
