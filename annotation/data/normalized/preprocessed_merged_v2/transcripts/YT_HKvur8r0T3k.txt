You'll never believe how easy it is to turn that old phone into a security camera. Why spend big bucks on a new security system when you can repurpose your old smartphone? First, download a free security camera app, like Alfred or ManyThing. These apps turn your phone's camera into a live feed that you can watch anytime. Next, find a good spot to place your phone. Think entryways, living rooms, or anywhere you want to keep an eye on. Use a phone stand or get creative with some household items to keep it steady. Plug your phone into a power source so it stays on 24 sevens. And don't forget to. connect it to Wi-Fi for a smooth, uninterrupted feed. Now sync the app with your current phone, and voila, you've got yourself a free functional security camera. So tech lovers, dust off that old phone and give it a new purpose.
