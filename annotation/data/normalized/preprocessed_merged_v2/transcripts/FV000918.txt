We went to the Spy Family Pop-Up Cafe in Ikebukuro. There was only bits of theming here and there, but the menu was on point with a wide variety of themed dishes. For mains, we ordered Becky's Meat Spaghetti, Damien's Steak Set, and Lloyd's Omurice Plate. For drinks, we had to get the Starlight on your drink. And how could we not order Bon's Tapioca Coconut Milk Drink? And for desserts, we ordered yours Rose Chocolate Fondant and Bon's Parfait. They also had life-size cutouts of the Forger Family for you to take pictures with, as well as limited edition merchandise just for this pop-up cafe. Which one of these would you want to try?
