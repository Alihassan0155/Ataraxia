Predator-free Whangare builds on decades of dedicated and successful community predator control so our tamariki and mokopuna may once again see our ngahere flourish and our taonga species thrive. We will work in partnership with mana whenua so that kaitiakitanga can be exercised for the area together. By spreading the kākono of knowledge further, we will empower Northlanders to get involved in predator control and enable native life to thrive. Tony
