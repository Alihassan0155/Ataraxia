This took me less than 20 minutes to make and a few simple ingredients. I love garlic fried rice But let's take it up a level and make it a pineapple garlic fried rice So start by cutting your fresh pineapple slice it and scoop out the inside dice these up and set these aside So for pineapple garlic fried rice, you need lots of garlic cook your garlic on medium heat for about one to two minutes Remove off the heat but keep the oil add your scallions and then two cups of cooked rice stir fry till the rice is separated Season with salt and soy sauce mix in your crispy garlic and your sweet pineapple Now you want to serve this directly in your pineapple bowl. And there you go, pineapple garlic fried rice. The pineapples add so much flavor. I love the touch of sweetness.
