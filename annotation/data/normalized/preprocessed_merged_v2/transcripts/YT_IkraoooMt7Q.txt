The most dangerous item in North Korea actually fits in your pocket. I met this North Korean woman who grew up with zero internet under a regime that forces people to think the outside world is evil. So watching Western media is a heavy crime. How do you know what's happening in the world? I don't know. Nothing? Nothing. People end up in brutal labor camps if they ever get caught. But deep inside North Korea's black markets, locals are secretly buying USB drives with American and South Korean movies or music. Despite the risk, my friend Evelyn bought them and watched in to find out about the outside world. And that inspired her to eventually escape the country.
