Before Scarlet and Violet drop with a new mechanic of Terracinalization, make sure you know all of these uses for Terra typings beyond getting new resistances and weaknesses. Grass types are immune to powder based moves, meaning they can't be affected by Sleep Powder, Spore, or Rage Powder. Dark types are immune to Prankster moves, so you can use this to gain immunity to things like Grimmsnarl Prankster Thunder Wave. Rock types gain a 50% special defense boost if Sandstorm is active. Steel, Ground, and Rock types are immune to Sandstorm damage. Ice types are immune to Hail damage. Ice types can't be trapped by Pokemon like Gothitelle, Electro types can't be paralyzed, Fire types can't be burned, and finally Poison types can't be poisoned or miss the move Toxic. Use these passive traits to your advantage when you're building your team and follow me for more competitive Pokemon content.
