Here are the top foods that you have to eat in Iraq. First up, we have Kibe. In the city of Mosul, it looks like a flatbread of rice and potato with minced meat. In Baghdad, it's more of a ball served with tomato sauce. Then there is Najafi Kima, an ancient dish from the city of Najaf, which is slow cooked for nearly eight hours. Iraqi Dolma are vegetables stuffed with rice, meat, herbs, and sweet and tangy flavors. And last but not least is Mazguf. This grilled carp is often considered the national dish of Iraq and you can get it almost anywhere.
