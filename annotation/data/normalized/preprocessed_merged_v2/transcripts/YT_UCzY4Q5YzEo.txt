Here's three food hacks I learned in restaurants. Hack number one. Buy a set of cheap squeeze bottles. Fill one or more with cooking oil and fill the others with your favorite sauces. Oil for easy access when cooking and sauces for easy drizzling when eating. Hack number two. Instead of putting your meal prep in one container, instead get yourself some deli containers, prep and cook your ingredients separately and store them separately. This is how restaurants keep things organized and fresher. Plus you can now mix and match a lot easier because it's separate. And hack number three. Whenever you're finished prepping all your ingredients out, when labeling, Take painter's tape in a single strip place in a cutting board, write out all of your labels using a knife, split them accordingly, and you have all your labels easily so you can date and label everything. That way, you're never guessing how long something's been living in your fridge.
