Murder by Numbers. Andy came up with the chords one night at dinner at Montserrat where we were recording Ghost of the Machine or one of them albums. And I'm sure that guy has more than ten fingers because that's what those voicings require. Sting already had this lyric for another song, but he threw that away and put them on here instead. It was an obscure track, only appearing on a B-side at first, but was always one of my favorites. The opening sax solo is made of those notes of those chords, those Andy chords, laid out horizontally.
