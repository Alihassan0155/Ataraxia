Disney is officially dead. In my eyes. We all know that next month, Wish is coming out. The 100 year anniversary celebration movie for Disney. I'm disappointed. As someone with a degree in animation, I look to Disney as what is supposed to be the pinnacle of animation. What is this movie? You see your consumers asking for you to make a new movie. You see your consumers asking you to go back to traditional 2D animation. You see your community asking for diverse characters and representation. And so you do. You give us a woman of color protagonist, but then you give her the same personality that we've seen in the last like four Disney princesses. You ignore the request for traditional 2D animation and you just say, oh, well, we made the backgrounds 2D, so it's like hybrid. That's not what we asked for. These movies did what nobody else could do. They told diverse stories with stunning music, with stunning animation. Wish isn't going to fulfill any of those marks. Your trailer looks unfinished, which makes me think your movie is going to look unfinished. I'm was growing up, Disney was magical and it made me want to be an animator, which is why I am one. This is supposed to be a celebration of all of those years of hard work and creativity and dedication. And instead of letting creative people make the creative decisions and make wonderful movies like they've proven that they can do over and over in the past, we have Disney executives coming in and telling these creative people what to make and what people will pay for. People will pay for good movies. Make a good movie.
