Our ash lumber is under attack! The emerald ash borer beetle is devastating these trees in these quarantine zones in Minnesota. Ash lumber is currently surprisingly cheap at the moment, knowing that the species may become extinct soon, and that means no more ash lumber for anybody. But there's hope! Dedicated conservation groups such as the Minnesota Department of Agriculture and the University of Minnesota are fighting to save these species from extinction.
