Researchers have probed deep into one of the greatest mysteries of the universe. Why, if it fits, I sits. In a study, researchers were able to get pet cats to sit on a 2D square marked on the floor like you've probably seen on the internet. But that's not all, because the cats also sat in an optical illusion of a square just as often. It's an illusion humans also see, which helps us understand cats' perception. And by the way, this was a citizen science study, meaning the researchers leveraged the power of internet cat pictures to recruit participants to do real science. They say this is a great new way to study cats because the kitties got to stay home where they're comfortable instead of being in a lab, so they probably acted more natural. So thanks internet, let's do some more science!
