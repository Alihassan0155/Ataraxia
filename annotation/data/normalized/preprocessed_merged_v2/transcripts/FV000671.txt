Leaving the kickoff as first man. Basically, the situation here is a lot of time on kickoff. The ball will 50-50 out to the side, and your teammate will be the one to scoop it up. What most people do in this situation is just instantly rotate back to corner boost and let their teammate have a 1v1. However, the thing you have to understand in these situations is that if one person completely gets control of the ball after kickoff, there's usually going to be a play on that really quick. So instead of getting boost, I always recommend you collect pads and follow the play, especially in 2v2, if there's going to be a shot on net after the 50-50. Just from doing this, I've been able to convert so many goals. So try this at your rank, and I promise you'll pick up free goals every game.
