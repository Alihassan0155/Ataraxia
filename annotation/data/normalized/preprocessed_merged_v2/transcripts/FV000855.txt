Sit down and relax at Small Worlds Tokyo. Not only do they have working space stations, but they also have a working airport where planes can take off at one runway and land on the opposite runway. And it was at this point that I realized that watching miniature airplanes take off was just as satisfying as watching the real ones. The airport itself is filled with a ton of detail, and even cars drive around the airport as if they're performing their daily tasks. And once you've watched a few take off...
