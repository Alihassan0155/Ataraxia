This is just a regular ice cube, and if I drop it into water... It floats. This is because as water freezes, it expands, and the density of the solid becomes less than the liquid. This on the other hand is another ice cube that I made, but this one's kind of special... because it's actually able to sink. This isn't because of some weird ice cube making trick though, and it's just because I made it using heavy water. This heavy water, which is also known as D2O, is about 11% denser than regular H2O. So, while it still expands when it freezes, this extra density allows it to still stay heavier than regular water, and I think that's pretty cool.
