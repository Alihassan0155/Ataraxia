Dubai, a city of luxury, innovation, and culture. If you're planning to visit, here are three must-see places you can't miss. First up, the Burj Khalifa. At 828 meters, it's the tallest building in the world. Head up to the observation deck for breathtaking views of the city. Next, the Dubai Mall. Whether you're shopping, dining, or enjoying the Dubai Fountain Show, it's a one-stop destination for everything luxury and entertainment. Finally, don't miss Palm Jumeirah. This iconic man-made island is home to the luxurious Atlantis.
