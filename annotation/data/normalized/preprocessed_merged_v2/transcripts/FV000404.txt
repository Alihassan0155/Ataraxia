Here are four shield fusions you must try in Tears of the Kingdom. The first one is the shield cart. This cart fusion is going to make traveling across areas so much easier. Who needs shield surfing when you can just have a skateboard? The shield cannon is a must-fuse item and is absolutely incredible. You can destroy tons of enemies with this and send entire squads of them flying with explosions while being able to protect yourself. Using a sapphire shield is broken. When an enemy attacks you, it freezes them and all you have to do is shield block. It's the ultimate defense. If you fuse a topaz to a shield, it'll electrocute the enemy without affecting you and makes them drop their weapons. Fusing the silver lino horn on your shield allows Link to become a dual wielder. Seriously, this one is so cool. Walk up to enemies and start shield bashing them along with your weapon to attack.
