Ever wonder where the most breathtaking vistas reside? Gaze upon the world's most scenic hiking trails. Start by stepping into the vibrant foliage of Japan's Mount Fuji, an iconic beauty that's more than just a postcard. Venture next to the rugged cliffs of Norway's Trolltunga where you'll feel on top of the world. Don't miss the Grand Canyon's Bright Angel Trail in the States, a desert wonder that tests your endurance. Finally, bask in the serene tranquility of New Zealand's Milford Track, a rainforest journey that's a feast for the eyes. Remember the best views come after the hardest climbs. Happy hiking!
