This isn't your typical safari. We just spent three nights on a luxury river cruise down the Chobe River between Namibia and Botswana. And honestly, this might be the best safari experience we've ever had. The boat was gorgeous, all-inclusive, and delicious. But the real magic? River safaris. Every day, we hopped on a smaller boat and got up close with the wild animals. We saw giant elephant families with the tiniest, cutest babies. Like, how does this baby even look cute while it's pooping. They were all drinking, splashing, even swimming across the river. There were crocs sunbathing nearby, hippos everywhere, which was eerie, but awesome. Baboons on the riverbank, an eagle perched in a tree, and even one lone giraffe. No bumpy jeeps, no dust. It was just calm, wild, and completely magical. I actually thought we were about to get a death.
