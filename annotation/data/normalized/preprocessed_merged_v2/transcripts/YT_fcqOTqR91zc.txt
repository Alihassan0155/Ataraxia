The interior of the 2019 Porsche Cayenne received a design overhaul. Generously used leather and glossy components definitely speak of luxury at the first glance. But the biggest change will have to be its center console and infotainment system. With most of its functions hidden, the new center console looks very simplistic. But some argued that the last generation center console looks and feels more mechanical and cooler. What do you think? Comment your opinions below. All the features in the center console come to life as soon as the car is turned on. Though it feels unique, the biggest downside is that the entire panel is a fingerprint magnet.
