We consume not because we need to consume We eat, we watch television We have a conversation Not because we need to do them because we don't want to go home and touch the suffering inside So even if the television program is not interesting enough We do not have the courage to turn it off kerana kita takut kita harus berhubung dengan kesakitan. Jadi, praktik nafas dan berjalan dengan nafas dapat membantu menghasilkan tenaga nafas supaya kita boleh pulang ke rumah, mengenali dan mengambil alih kesakitan kita dan sembuh. Itulah ajaran Buddha.
