The Camwood bat is a heavy training bat, but the weight is added right above the hands, which makes the bat feel lighter than it actually is. So the Camwood bat allows you to train with a heavy bat without breaking down your mechanics while increasing your bat speed and your power. That is why Mo's nine-year-old son, Lee, here is able to train with a 35-ounce bat with no issues. This bat is the best teaching tool for creating bat speed, bat path, and fast hands.
