Let's look at three techniques that are really helpful for vagal nerve activation. The final technique is to double the length of your exhale. When we breathe slowly, specifically when we emphasize the exhale portion of the breath, that means our diaphragm spends more time relaxed, more time snuggled up against our heart. Those baroreceptors via our vagus nerve communicate to our brain to slow everything down. These three techniques, ocean breath, chin lock, doubling the length of our exhale, these are really, really powerful techniques when combined can have a very, very quick self-soothing effect to relieve stress and anxiety.
