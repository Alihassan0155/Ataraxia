Music So welcome to ANU's Heavy Ion Accelerator Facility, home of Australia's Experimental Nuclear Physics Research Program. At the heart of the facility is a 15 million volt tandem van de graaf particle accelerator. We also have a superconducting linear booster accelerator and a whole suite of experimental equipment that's designed to perform detailed experiments with accelerated ion beams. Scientists based in Australia and from across the world come to the facility every year to perform experiments that address a number of key questions in nuclear science. Some of these include what happens when nuclei fuse together in nuclear fission. ANU is the only university in Australia who have a huge accelerator, so that's why I chose ANU.
