Withered Foxy can actually talk in FNAF 2? Well, kind of. Let me explain. In FNAF 2, we all know the main animatronics. Those including the toy animatronics, Withered animatronics, and Balloon Boy's dummy. And while playing FNAF 2, the only animatronic that can talk is Balloon Boy. So then how can Withered Foxy talk? Well, when the FNAF 2 trailer released in 2014, there was a very popular rumor based around Withered Foxy's jump scare, where some people swore that they heard him talk.
