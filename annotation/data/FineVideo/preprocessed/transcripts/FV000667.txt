How to start fire with flint and steel. This is your flint or chert, this is your steel, not stainless, and this is your char cloth. In this case it's paper towel. Easiest way to do it and if you need more information please click the link below because I cannot fit everything you need to know in this one video. Take your char cloth, hold it with your thumb up against a sharp edge of your flint and strike the steel down towards it. Once you notice you've got an ember, blow on it just to get it established. I like to hold it together with the finger. I take that flint and I put it into a tinder bundle and char cloth and I put this all together. You can stick a whole bunch of stuff in there including dry lint. You hold this all together, I put my kindling right there with it and I blow. And there's your fire.
