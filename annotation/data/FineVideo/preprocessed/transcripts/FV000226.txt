Ever feel pain in your ankles after running? You're not alone. Running is a high-impact physical activity. Stress on weight-bearing joints such as knees and ankles is unavoidable. This is especially common in athletes. You can help reduce ankle pain while running by learning and consistently practicing proper running form, wearing proper shoes, avoiding uneven surfaces, being aware if you're putting more strain on your ankles with sudden changes in speed or direction. Happy running!
