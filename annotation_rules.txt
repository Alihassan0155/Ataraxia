ATARAXIA HUMAN ANNOTATION — QUICK GUIDE

Goal: Judge the likely immediate emotional effect of the COMPLETE video on a
typical adult viewer. Do not label only the creator's emotion, one scene, or
your personal reaction.

IMPORTANT

- Watch the full video with sound, then replay unclear parts if needed.
- Do not view Gemini or Qwen results before submitting your labels.
- Direct video/audio evidence has highest priority.
- Transcripts may contain errors. Metadata captions have LOW importance.
- Never assign a label from the caption alone.

FIELDS TO SCORE

1. Valence (0 to 10)
   0 = extremely unpleasant, 5 = neutral, 10 = extremely pleasant.

2. Arousal (0 to 10)
   0 = very calm/unstimulating, 5 = moderate, 10 = extremely intense.
   Arousal is separate from valence: intense content can be pleasant or
   unpleasant.

3. Emotion weights (must total 10)
   Divide exactly 10 points among ALL eight emotions below. Use 0 when an
   emotion is absent. More than one emotion can receive points.

   joy                 ___  happiness, amusement, delight
   sadness             ___  sorrow, grief, disappointment
   anger               ___  irritation, frustration, hostility
   fear_anxiety        ___  fear, worry, tension, unease
   surprise            ___  shock, startle, unexpectedness
   disgust             ___  revulsion, aversion, strong distaste
   calm_contentment    ___  peace, comfort, gentle satisfaction
   neutral             ___  little or no clear emotional effect
                       ---
   TOTAL               10

   Example: joy 6 + surprise 2 + neutral 2 + all other emotions 0 = 10.

4. Dominant emotion
   Select the emotion with the highest weight.

5. Recovery suitability (0 to 10)
   Would this be a good video after several intense or overstimulating videos?
   0 = unsuitable, 5 = mixed, 10 = highly soothing/restorative.
   This is not simply 10 minus arousal.

6. Confidence (0 to 10)
   0 = very uncertain, 5 = moderately certain, 10 = very certain.

7. Evidence and notes
   Briefly record observable visual, audio, speech/text, and motion evidence.
   Note mixed emotions, unclear language, missing audio, or other ambiguity.

FINAL CHECK

- You watched the complete video.
- All scores are between 0 and 10.
- The eight emotion weights total exactly 10.
- Dominant emotion has the highest weight.
- No unavailable evidence was invented.
- Model predictions were not viewed.

If multiple people annotate, work independently and discuss disagreements only
after everyone has submitted their original labels.
